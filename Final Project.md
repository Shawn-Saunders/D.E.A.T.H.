## Business Case Selection 

Our project will be a fitness application featuring a patented Death Calendar™. The fitness app will collect health data from a selection of other health apps, note your geographic area, and users will fill out a questionnaire to determine their projected time of death.  

## Good Health and Well-Being 

The fitness application will promote healthy lifestyle changes by highlighting the user’s own habits and its relation to the Death Calendar™. This will provide negative feedback to incentivize a healthier lifestyle and recommend more sustainable alternatives to increase the time left on the Death Calendar™.  

Target 3.5 “Strengthens the prevention and treatment of substance abuse including narcotic drug abuse and harmful use of alcohol” 
The app will be discouraging drug abuse and usage of Substances through it effecting the persons death projection if it becomes a habit they record inside the app.  

The app could provide recommendations to services for those who are struggling with addiction, such as rehabilitation and psychological (Psychosocial) care. 

The app should Record how much Alcohol they consume within a calendar year for the SDGS 3.5.2

## Sustainable Cities and Communities 

Target 11.4 "Strengthen efforts to protect and safeguard the world’s cultural and natural heritage" 

The Death Calendar™ will provide daily facts about different burial practices around the world. This will spread cultural awareness about these cultures and encourage users to be curious and seek out more information about them. 

Target 11.6 “Reduce the adverse per capita environmental impact of cities, including by paying special attention to air quality and municipal and other waste management”  

The Death Calendar™ will account for environmental facts like air pollution in relation to the user’s lifespan to bring forward awareness to different eco-initiatives in their community that they can participate in. This will promote a sustainable community by giving incentive to reducing carbon emissions and following safe waste practices. 

Target 11.7 “Provide universal access to safe, inclusive and accessible green and public spaces, in particular for women and children, older persons, and persons with disabilities.”  

D.E.A.T.H. will inform users about local parks and recreational areas near them, accounting for disabilities and age by displaying different transport options they can access for their convenience. This way, people are knowledgeable about public spaces where they can follow trails, exercise, and meet other people that will encourage a healthier lifestyle.  

## Climate action 

SGD 13 climate action target 13.3 states that the system should "Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change mitigation, adaptation, impact reduction and early warning".  

Our app is actively educating users on climate actions they can take while they are both alive, by actively supporting ecologically sound organizations or volunteering for local eco-initiatives. Which directly targets this specific goal 

D.E.A.T.H. will educate users on many different after-death care options that are sustainable for the environment and leave a positive impact. One example is human composting, where the body is decomposed into nutrient rich soil to plant a tree, allowing the body to naturally decompose in an environmentally friendly way while providing trees to future generations.
