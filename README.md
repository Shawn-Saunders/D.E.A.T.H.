# D.E.A.T.H.
A project for System Development 2. Our project is a mobile fitness app with a focus on your projected date of death.

## What is D.E.A.T.H.
D.E.A.T.H. is an acronym translating to Detailed Educational Athletic Thought Helper. We are working to build a fitness app that calculates you projected lifetime based on lifestyle, eating choices, and the environment where you live. Some factors increase life expectancy and others decrease it. 

The purpose of the app is to incentivize green living, getting involved in cleaning up your town, and living a healthy lifestyle. All while promoting green after-death care and partnering with company's that would ecologically break down your body and plant trees, or return you to the Earth in a healthy way. We would also educate users on different cultures and religions using a DOTD (Death of the Day) message, which are cool facts about lesser known after-death options, famous deaths on that day, and general healthy living facts.

The app would target a more macabre market, particularly people who find positive reinforcement to be less effective. Our app would use a dry sense of humor about their projected death date, such as if the user ate a lot of sweets it would ask "was that worth a day of your life?".
