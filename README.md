# D.E.A.T.H.
A project for System Development 2. Our project is a mobile fitness app with a focus on your projected date of death.


## Assignment description
This project, like your larger MGMT1223 assignments, focuses on designing a solution to a business problem. The problem must be small, realistic, and offer tangible business or societal value. Cases require approval from your professor, who will act as your Project Sponsor and advisor. Regular consultations with them are encouraged.

Your goal is to produce planning and object-oriented requirements documentation (listed below), demonstrating your engagement with a real-world problem and your ability to draft the design an object-oriented solution. These are highly in demand skills in IT that go beyond writing code.

The project consists of five key deliverables submitted throughout the semester, culminating in a Final Report. Deliverables will be graded based on your group’s ability to provide objective evidence of consistent progress. Use the same templates used in MGMT1223.

While deliverables for groups are presented collectively at report meetings, members are graded individually on their timely, meaningful contributions. Deliverable items will be assigned a formative grade to guide students, meaning they are reassessed in the final report in case any improvements are made in response to provided feedback.
